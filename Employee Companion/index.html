📝 Lobby Tasks & Functions
As an employee you have 5 tasks to do when you are in lobby duty. (Note: Selling Insurance is for HCs only)
  • Selling Medkits: Flex 'G' muscle -> Organization -> Sell A Medkit. (Max 50 at a time)
  • Selling Insurance: Flex 'G' muscle -> Organization -> Sell Insurance.
  • Heal A Player: Flex 'Z' muscle directly on the player.
  • Checking Illness: Flex 'G' muscle -> Organization -> Learn a player's Illness.
  • Selling Pills: Flex 'G' muscle -> Organization -> Sell the pill for the disease. (Max 20 Pills at a time)
    Mild = 1 Pill, Progressive = 2 Pills, Severe = 3 Pills.

⚠️ Special Situations Guide
Stuck Under Car ▼
Ram the vehicle with your ambulance maximum 3 times to dislodge them. If unsuccessful, immediately call Admin on tickets (Code B situation).
Patient Underwater ▼
Swim down to confirm the patient is physically there. Return to solid ground, take a screenshot, and call Admin on tickets to teleport them to the shore.
Mid-Air / Flying Patient ▼
Do not attempt to patch mid-air. Take a screenshot, report as Code B, and call Admin to teleport the patient safely to the ground.
Beach Market Dispatch ▼
Ambulances and vehicles are prohibited from entering the Beach Market. Park outside, walk inside, and patch only if you have an extra medkit in your personal inventory.
Black Market Dispatch ▼
Only patch patients located outside the red border line. Never enter on-duty. Only LEOs can order on-duty EMS to leave.
